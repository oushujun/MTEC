Methods
-------
The B73 calls come from the LTR promoter project (/data/shujun/projects/LTR_promoter_ML).
B73 = B73.PLATINUM.pseudomolecules-v1.

1. PBS/PPT on the library's LTR entries (600)
   - The two LTRs of an entry are its longest direct terminal repeat: >= 100 bp at >= 80% identity,
     starting within 20 bp of both ends, with the boundaries snapped to TG...CA. 564 of the 600 entries
     have one.
   - Each entry is scanned in both orientations with gt ltrdigest 1.6.5 (default parameters). The tRNA
     library is the LTR_FINDER grass tRNA genes with CCA added (745).
   - The project's calibrated rules:
       PBS: edit distance <= 1, length >= 11, <= 5 bp from the 5' LTR, <= 2 bp from the tRNA 3' end.
       PPT: >= 8 bp, ending 2 bp inside to 10 bp before the 3' LTR.
       When both orientations qualify, the higher-quality one must lead by >= 2.
   - Precision is 99.6% on 33,768 B73 intact elements whose annotated strand and domains agree.
   - In the library, it agrees with TEsorter on 387 of 389 entries.

2. Homology to strand-called B73 copies (BLAST+ 2.17.0 dc-megablast, e-value 1e-10)
   a. Each LTR entry is searched against 51,730 B73 intact LTR elements whose strand rests on their own
      sequence: domains (46,020) or PBS/PPT alone (5,710). An element whose PBS/PPT contradicts its domain
      strand is dropped.
      - A hit counts only where the entry's own LTR meets a reference LTR. This way a TE nested in the entry,
        or in the reference, cannot speak for it.
      - A strand decides with >= 90% of the bitscore.
      - Agreement with TEsorter: 430/430. On PBS/PPT-only references it is 204/204, including 92/92 calls
        resting on one reference.
      - Agreement with the entry's own PBS/PPT: 418/419. The miss is liove_AC207121_9462, where PBS/PPT is
        the one that is wrong.
   b. Entries without terminal repeats (36): a reference element that holds >= 50% of the entry within its
      LTR zone. Agreement with TEsorter: 443/443.
   c. LTR entries that nothing above orients: PBS/PPT is called, as in method 1, on full-length B73 copies
      OUTSIDE the intact set.
      - The copies are dc-megablast chains that cover >= 80% of the entry and reach both of its ends.
      - Intact elements are excluded, and at most 50 copies are kept per entry.
      - A call needs >= 90% of the called copies to agree.
      - On 40 TEsorter-stranded entries: 102/103 copies and 20/20 entries agree.
   Homology from internal regions alone (no LTR involved) never orients an entry, and never overrules
   TEsorter.

3. Helitrons: 5'-TC...CTRR-3' as deposited = +, and its reverse complement = -. 10 of the 11 Helitrons have
   the motif; Hip1_14, an 80-bp fragment, does not.


Decision rules (LTR entries)
----------------------------
  - TEsorter leads.
    - Where the entry's PBS/PPT disagrees, homology breaks the tie. naasuj_AC211569_11281 and
      liove_AC207121_9462 keep TEsorter (+).
    - Internal-only homology cannot overturn it: nida_AC206942_9401 keeps TEsorter (+).
  - Without TEsorter, PBS/PPT decides, unless homology, IsoClassifier or E4 disagrees.
  - Without either, homology decides, in the order 2a/2b, then 2c, unless IsoClassifier or E4 disagrees.
  - Any disagreement these rules do not settle leaves the entry ?. That is one entry: vegu_AC190718_85, a
    PPT-only + against homology -.
